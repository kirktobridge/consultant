## Value

a paraphrased criterion is a quietly edited definition of done, and today nothing
compares the report to the entry. Warn first, so we can see whether the warnings are
true before anything is refused.

## Scope

- `scripts/close-report.mjs --check <ID>` compares each Accept criterion of the
  entry as it stands on the trunk (whitespace-normalized) with the branch's
  close-report commit(s), and lists each criterion missing, altered, or without a
  verdict.
- /land runs it and puts its warnings in the handshake. It never stops a landing.
- Ad-hoc branches, which have no entry, are skipped.
- Out: refusing a landing (enforcement is a later entry); judging whether a verdict is
  right.

## Accept

- A landing whose report reworded one criterion shows a warning naming that criterion
  in the handshake, and the landing still completes.
- A report quoting every criterion with a verdict produces no warning.
- Line wrapping and indentation differences alone don't warn.

## Notes

Codification program, Phase 1 (item B3, warn mode). Before enforcement (B4) is filed,
the warnings over the program's real landings are tallied to see whether they were
true. Plan: no — warn only.
