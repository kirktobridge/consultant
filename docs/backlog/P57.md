## Value

The staleness check and groom.mjs can see a bug fix land after an entry that depends on
it was drafted. Today they can't: the bugs register has no closing date, while the
other three registers do (verified 2026-10-24 in `scripts/entries.mjs`).

## Scope

- In: a `fixed: YYYY-MM-DD` header key, in the bugs README, `scripts/entries.mjs`, the
  schema check and groom.mjs, together. Every fixed bug gets its date backfilled from
  git.
- Out: a date for `wontfix`.

## Accept

- The schema check fails a `fixed` bug that has no `fixed:` date.
- An open entry drafted before a dependency bug's `fixed:` date is flagged stale, and groom.mjs lists it.

Plan: yes — it changes a register's format, its checker and groom.mjs.
