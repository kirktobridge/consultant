## Value

A spike can be accepted through ship.mjs. Today the schema check refuses the `shipped`
flip and the Resolution one at a time, so it stays a hand edit.

## Scope

`ship.mjs shipped <P#> --owner … --resolves-as verified|refuted|moot --from <file>` on an
entry carrying `resolves: A-n`: both entries and the queue in one commit, then the
sweep. A spike accepted without `--resolves-as` is refused, naming the assumption.

## Accept

- Accepting a spike with `--resolves-as` makes one commit holding the spike, its assumption and the queue, and the schema check is green.
- Accepting a spike without it is refused, names the A-n, and changes nothing.
