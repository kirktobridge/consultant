## Value

the report I accept against quotes my criteria exactly, because code copies them, not
a session retyping them at the end of a build.

## Scope

- `scripts/close-report.mjs <ID>` prints the skeleton from the trunk's entry:
  each Accept criterion word for word, with an empty verdict (observed / not observed /
  not exercised) and evidence slot, and the REVIEW line slot. A bug gets its Repro and
  Expected instead.
- /implement's close step fills the skeleton instead of writing the report from
  scratch.
- Out: checking the filled report (P44).

## Accept

- The skeleton's criteria match the entry's Accept bullets word for word, one per
  criterion.
- A bug's skeleton carries its Repro and Expected, not Accept.
- An unknown ID, or an entry with no Accept, exits 2 saying which, rather than
  printing an empty skeleton.
