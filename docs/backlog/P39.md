## Value

when a landing stops because a conflict was resolved to one side, I get the builder's
reason and its suggested answer, so I answer yes or no instead of reading git's
conflict regions.

## Scope

- `land.mjs continue`, on a region resolved to exactly one side, requires the session's
  rationale for each such region before it exits 4. The stop prints, per region: the
  file, both sides, the side kept, the rationale and the suggested answer.
- The rationale is kept in the landing record and appears in /land's report.
- /implement's close and /orchestrate's `QUESTION` relay ask in that yes/no form. A
  "no" leaves the rebase in progress for the session to resolve the region again.
- Out: what the check detects (a blend still passes, per D-4); skipping the stop.

## Accept

- When a builder keeps one side of a conflict, the landing stops and shows me, for each
  such region, what each side had, what was kept, why, and the builder's suggested
  answer.
- The builder can't reach that stop without writing the reason: `land.mjs` refuses and
  names the region.
- Answering yes lands it, with the reason in the landing report; answering no sends the
  builder back to resolve that region again.
