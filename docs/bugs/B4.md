## Repro

On main after P17 landed (in-progress, awaiting acceptance), `node scripts/next.mjs
--count 3` printed `skipped: P17 — claimed (in-progress): another session is
building it`. "That reason is out of date for P17 now. It has landed and is
waiting on you."

## Expected

An entry that has landed and is waiting on the owner is skipped with a reason
saying so, not as another session's build.
