## Options

(a) Keep today's rule (the owner's choice in P15, 2026-10-19): a resolved region that
is identical to one side stops the landing (`land.mjs` exit 4) for the owner.
(b) Rationale instead of a stop: the session records, in the landing record, why each
one-sided region is right; the landing goes on, and the handshake shows each such
region with its rationale, so the owner accepts or rejects it with the entry.
(c) Keep the stop, but cheaper to answer: the session attaches its rationale and
recommended resolution, so the owner answers yes or no instead of reading regions.

## Context

The check is a syntactic stand-in for "both entries' intent survived", and it errs both
ways: a correct one-sided resolution (both entries made the same fix; one supersedes
the other) stops, and a blend that keeps both sides' lines but breaks one's intent
passes (P15's notes: a blended resolution passed the check and only the suite caught
it). Under /orchestrate a stop holds the land lock, so every other landing waits on
the owner. Raised by the codification review, 2026-10-21.

## Decided

(c): the stop stays; the builder brings its rationale and a suggested answer, so the
owner answers yes or no.

## Why

The product owner's choice, 2026-10-21. A person still looks at every one-sided
resolution, which catches what the check can't, but the look costs seconds instead of
reading raw conflicts.

## Reopen if

Under /orchestrate, stops pile up enough that the paused landings cost more than
reviewing after the fact. That would be option (b).
