## Options

(a) wait until photocull has run one measure, taken one decision and resolved one
assumption under the new shapes; (b) waive the wait and port now.

## Decided

(b): waive the wait and port now.

## Why

"We can do this ourselves" (the product owner, 2026-10-16). The change had landed in
photocull (all seven porting commits present); only the post-port exercise was missing:
one decision had been taken there, but no assumption had resolved and no measure run.

## Reopen if

the port turns up a shape that photocull's use would have caught: a field nobody
fills, or a check that fires on correct entries.
